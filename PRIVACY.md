# Privacy Policy — QLCT

**Last updated: 24/05/2026**

> This Markdown file is the source-of-truth summary in English. The full, 5-language version (vi/en/es/zh/ja) lives in `privacy_policy.html` and is published via GitHub Pages.

This Privacy Policy describes how the QLCT app ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Information stored locally on your device
- Financial transactions you record (amount, category, wallet, date, notes)
- Wallets, categories, budgets, savings goals, debts
- Net-worth assets and liabilities
- App settings (language, persona, theme, security preferences)

**This data never leaves your device** unless you explicitly export it (Backup / CSV) or opt in to Android's Auto Backup to your private Google Drive.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Advertising identifier**: provided by Android, used by AdMob to serve ads. Resettable in device settings.
- **Approximate location** (via IP / timezone): used by AdMob to serve regionally relevant ads.
- **Basic device info** (model, OS version): used by AdMob for compatibility.

We do NOT collect: your name, email, phone number, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, or any personally identifiable information. We operate no server, so there is no analytics, no crash reporting, and no telemetry sent to us.

## 2. Third-party services

QLCT uses two third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

- **Google AdMob** (advertising): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **fawazahmed0/exchange-api** (foreign-exchange rates, no user data sent): https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api

## 3. How we use information

- To provide and maintain the app (entirely on-device).
- To show advertisements (AdMob).
- To fetch reference foreign-exchange rates when you open the Exchange screen.

## 4. Children's privacy

QLCT is designed for adults aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us.

## 5. Your rights

You can:
- **Delete all your data** at any time via Settings → Erase all data.
- **Export your data** via Settings → Backup, which gives you a CSV/JSON file you can keep.
- **Opt out of personalized ads** via Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

## 6. Data retention

- Locally stored data: kept until you delete the app or clear data manually.
- We retain no server-side data because we operate no server.

## 7. International users (GDPR/EU)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under GDPR. Because we do not collect or process any personal data on a server, there is no controller-side data to access, port, or delete on our side. For data processed by AdMob, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Significant changes will be announced in-app.
